# Safety Considerations — Industrial Compressor Maintenance Documentation System

## Stored Pressure

Compressors and air receivers store compressed air that can cause serious injury if released suddenly.

- Isolate and vent the system before any maintenance.
- Verify zero pressure with a gauge before opening any connection.
- Do not rely on valves alone; use a venting procedure.

## High Temperature

Compressor oil and air can reach 100 °C+.

- Allow the unit to cool before maintenance.
- Wear heat-resistant gloves.
- Beware of hot discharge pipework.

## Electrical Hazard

Compressor motors are typically 3-phase mains.

- Apply lockout-tagout before any electrical work.
- Verify dead with a calibrated voltage tester.

## Rotating Parts

Compressor airends and cooling fans have rotating parts that can entangle clothing.

- Ensure guards are in place before starting.
- Do not wear loose clothing near running compressors.

## Oil Hazard

Compressor oil is slippery and is an environmental hazard if spilled.

- Use drip trays.
- Dispose of used oil at a designated recycling point.
- Clean up spills immediately with absorbent material.

## Air Receiver Inspection

Air receiver inspection is regulated in most jurisdictions.

- EU: Pressure Equipment Directive (PED) 2014/68/EU
- US: ASME Section VIII
- Sri Lanka: Factories Ordinance pressure vessel regulations

Inspections must be performed by an authorised inspector at the required intervals. This framework does not include air receiver inspection records; they must be maintained separately per local regulations.
