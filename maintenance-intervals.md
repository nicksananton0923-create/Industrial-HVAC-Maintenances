# Compressor Maintenance Intervals — Industrial Compressor Maintenance Documentation System

## Overview

This document describes typical OEM maintenance intervals for industrial air compressors. The intervals are typical values for portfolio demonstration; always verify against the specific compressor's OEM manual.

## Rotary Screw Compressor

| Inspection / service | Interval | Run-hour basis |
|---|---|---|
| Visual inspection (oil level, leaks, noise) | Daily | — |
| Air filter inspection | Weekly | — |
| Oil level check | Weekly | — |
| Cooler cleaning | Monthly | — |
| Oil sample | Quarterly | 1000 h |
| Air filter replacement | 6-monthly | 2000 h |
| Oil change | — | 4000–8000 h |
| Oil filter change | — | 4000 h |
| Separator replacement | — | 4000–8000 h |
| Valve inspection | Annually | — |
| Air quality test (ISO 8573) | Annually | — |
| Major overhaul | — | 24,000–48,000 h |

## Reciprocating Compressor

| Inspection / service | Interval |
|---|---|
| Visual inspection | Daily |
| Oil level check | Daily |
| Air filter inspection | Weekly |
| Valve inspection | 6-monthly |
| Air filter replacement | Annually |
| Oil change | 6-monthly / 2000 h |
| Air quality test | Annually |
| Major overhaul (pistons, rings, bearings) | — 10,000–20,000 h |

## Centrifugal Compressor

| Inspection / service | Interval |
|---|---|
| Visual inspection | Daily |
| Vibration monitoring | Continuous (online) or monthly (offline) |
| Oil sample | Monthly |
| Cooler cleaning | Quarterly |
| Air quality test | Annually |
| Gearbox inspection | — 24,000 h |
| Major overhaul | — 48,000–96,000 h |

## Disclaimer

These intervals are typical values from common OEM manuals. Always verify against the specific compressor's OEM manual. Intervals may be shorter for harsh environments (high dust, high humidity, high ambient temperature) and longer for clean environments.
